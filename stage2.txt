Add a rule-based competition assessment to the app.

After a successful search, calculate and display above the results table:
- the total number of matching venues found within the 1km radius (all of them, not just the 5 shown)
- the distance in metres to the closest matching venue
- a competition level based on this rule:
  0-1 venues  -> "Lower observed direct competition"
  2-4 venues  -> "Moderate observed direct competition"
  5 or more   -> "Higher observed direct competition"
- if the closest venue is under 250 metres, add a separate note that a direct competitor is very nearby

Write it as a plain function that takes the count and the closest distance and returns the assessment text. Do not use an LLM for this. Keep it transparent and readable.

Also display the entered address, the selected category and the radius above the results.
